# Do and don't

## Do

1. Use `primary-500` gold as a fill or accent, and `accent-text` for gold-family text on light grounds.
2. Show Call and WhatsApp on every page, in the header, hero, CTA band and the floating button.
3. Keep every image slot on its fixed aspect ratio, using the placeholder until the photo exists.
4. Write components with logical properties so one markup serves French and Arabic.
5. Isolate phone numbers and e-mail addresses with `.lc-ltr` inside Arabic text.

## Don't

1. Don't set gold text on cream or paper: `primary-500` on `cream` is 2.66:1.
2. Don't use WhatsApp green anywhere except the WhatsApp buttons, and don't put white text on it.
3. Don't animate placeholders, or add autoplay, loops, parallax or spinners.
4. Don't add gradients, decorative illustration, emoji or heavy shadows.
5. Don't redraw, recolor or rebuild the logo: it is the single source of truth for the identity.
