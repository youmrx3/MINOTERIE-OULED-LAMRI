# Page templates

Home, Products and Contact are built only from the components in this system, at 1440px and 390px, in French (LTR) and Arabic (RTL). Image slots use the placeholder.

- **Home:** header, hero, strengths band, product cards, about teaser, testimonials, CTA band, footer, floating WhatsApp. The mobile template shows one product card and drops the about and testimonials sections to keep the page short; both belong on mobile in production.
- **Products:** header, page head (`h1`), product section blocks with alternating image side, CTA band, footer.
- **Contact:** header, page head, form card beside the contact info card, hours table and static map, footer. The mobile template drops the hours table.
- Previews live under Templates in the Components view, as HomeDesktopFR, HomeDesktopAR, HomeMobile and so on.
