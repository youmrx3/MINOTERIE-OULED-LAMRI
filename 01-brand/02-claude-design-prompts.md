## Prompt A — "Dove & Ear"

```
Design a logo identity for "LA COLOMBE", the consumer brand of SARL Minoterie
Ouled Lamri, a flour mill in Bordj Bou Arréridj, Algeria. Bilingual brand:
French/Latin and Arabic.

CONCEPT
A single dove in flight, seen from the side, with a clean geometric build.
The dove's wing is formed by the grains of a wheat ear: 5 to 7 grain shapes
fanning out as feathers, so the two ideas become one symbol. Wings slightly
raised, body compact, head forward. Nothing decorative beyond that.

STYLE
Modern, calm, premium food brand. Flat vector, solid fills, consistent curve
radius, optical balance. No gradients, no shadows, no outlines thinner than
the thinnest grain. Must stay readable at 32 px and at 40 px height.

TYPOGRAPHY
Wordmark "LA COLOMBE" in a refined serif with generous letter-spacing,
medium weight. Below it, smaller: "MINOTERIE OULED LAMRI" in a clean
sans-serif, uppercase, wide tracking. Arabic wordmark "الحمامة" in a sturdy
contemporary Naskh or Kufi style, optically matched in weight to the Latin.

PALETTE
Wheat gold #C08A2E, earth brown #4A3B2A, cream #F5EFE6. Maximum these three.

DELIVER ON ONE CANVAS
1. Primary horizontal lockup (symbol left, wordmark right)
2. Stacked lockup (symbol above wordmark)
3. Symbol alone, plus a simplified 32 px favicon version
4. Arabic horizontal lockup (symbol on the right, mirrored layout)
5. One-color versions: brown on cream, white on brown, black on white
6. Three small applications: website header bar, round social avatar,
   printed flour bag front (flat mockup, no photo)
Show the palette with hex codes under the lockups.
```

## Prompt B — "Millstone Seal"

```
Design a logo identity for "LA COLOMBE", the consumer brand of SARL Minoterie
Ouled Lamri, a flour mill in Bordj Bou Arréridj, Algeria. Bilingual brand:
French/Latin and Arabic.

CONCEPT
A circular seal. Outer ring carries the text "MINOTERIE OULED LAMRI" along the
top arc and "BORDJ BOU ARRÉRIDJ" along the bottom arc, separated by two small
wheat grains. Inside the ring: a dove, wings open, placed over a millstone
whose radial grooves (furrows) fan out behind it. The grooves are bold and
limited in number (12 to 16) so they survive printing and embossing.

STYLE
Traditional and trustworthy, craft and industrial heritage, like a quality
mark stamped on a bag of flour. Flat vector, solid shapes, strong contrast,
thick strokes. No gradients, no shadows, no fine textures. Must still work as
a single-color stamp.

TYPOGRAPHY
Ring text in a sturdy slab or transitional serif, uppercase, evenly tracked.
Main wordmark "LA COLOMBE" placed beside or below the seal in the same family,
bold. Arabic wordmark "الحمامة" with matching weight and stroke contrast.

PALETTE
Earth brown #4A3B2A as the dominant color, wheat gold #C08A2E for the dove or
the grooves, cream #F5EFE6 as background. Maximum these three.

DELIVER ON ONE CANVAS
1. Full seal with ring text
2. Seal + "LA COLOMBE" wordmark, horizontal and stacked
3. Simplified mark without ring text (dove + millstone only), plus a 32 px
   favicon version
4. Arabic lockup with the ring text mirrored for right-to-left reading
5. One-color versions: brown on cream, white on brown, black on white, and a
   single-color stamp/emboss version
6. Three small applications: website header bar, round social avatar,
   printed flour bag front (flat mockup, no photo)
Show the palette with hex codes under the lockups.
```

## Follow-up prompts (use after the first generation)

**Simplify for small sizes**
```
Take the selected option and produce a simplified symbol that stays legible at
24, 32 and 48 px. Remove detail rather than shrinking it. Show all three sizes
on cream and on brown.
```

**Arabic polish**
```
Refine the Arabic lockup. Adjust the weight, baseline and spacing of "الحمامة"
so it feels drawn together with the Latin wordmark, not translated. Show it
beside the Latin version at equal visual height.
```

**Charter alignment (when the client sends his colors)**
```
Recolor the selected option with the official palette: [primary hex],
[secondary hex], [accent hex]. Keep every proportion and shape unchanged. Check
contrast of the wordmark on each background and adjust only if it fails.
```

**Presentation board for the client**
```
Create a one-page presentation board for the selected logo: primary lockup,
symbol, palette with hex codes, one-color versions, and header / avatar / bag
applications. Clean cream background, no extra decoration.
```
